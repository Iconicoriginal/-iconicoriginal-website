Our story

The most beautiful thing in the world? Getting to know it.

Our story was born from the desire to travel, from the pleasure we take in observing beautiful things. From the need to meet people, ideas and lifestyles different from our own, with the aim of enriching ourselves with knowledge. Drawing inspiration from Berlin street art, feeling vertigo inside Cologne Cathedral, hearing the music of the Glockenspiel in Munich, listening to the legend of the Wörthersee.

From those journeys a craft was born: bringing transformation wherever it is needed, without asking spaces to give up their own history. Today our sites speak Italian, French, German, Greek and Hebrew — and home is still where it all began: Val di Zoldo, in the Belluno Dolomites.

Not an installer.
A director.

A company with an in-house crew sells its workers' hours, where those workers live. We sell a result: we design the intervention, choose the materials, build the right team for that specific site — 3M-certified installers, drywallers, electricians, floor layers — and direct it through to handover. Just as an architect doesn't pour concrete, we don't need a fixed crew to guarantee the work. Quite the opposite: that freedom is what lets us work anywhere.
