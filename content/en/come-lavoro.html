The method

One point of contact,
anywhere.

Other installers sell the crew they keep in-house, within the radius that crew can travel. Iconic Original works the other way round: it starts from the client's space and builds the site around it — designers, selected installers, craftsmen — wherever that space is. Not a limitation: the model.

The difference

Just as an architect
doesn't pour concrete.

Nobody asks an architect to mix mortar: they ask them to answer for the building. That is exactly Iconic Original's role: it takes care of the design, selects the 3M DI-NOC finishes surface by surface, picks installers with the relevant experience for each intervention and directs the works through to handover.

For the client this changes everything: no chains of anonymous subcontractors, no responsibilities bouncing around. One point of contact follows schedule, quality and result, from idea to opening.

The five phases
of the direction.

01

Survey and listeningOn site or remotely

The first step is seeing the space — in person, or through measurements and photographs when the site is on the other side of Europe. What must stay, what must change, how the business operates: the design starts there, not from a catalogue.

02

Design and specificationEvery detail decided first

The intervention is defined surface by surface: what gets covered, what gets built, what gets integrated — drywall, lighting, floating floors where needed. The client approves a clear design, with fixed schedule and costs, before the site opens.

03

Material selection3M DI-NOC, sample in hand

The 3M DI-NOC finishes — woods, metals, stones, concretes, the sustainable E-Series RC and the abrasion-resistant AR series — are selected on the sample and checked on site, in the real light of the space. Only first-choice materials, installed to standard.

04

Coordinated works, anywhereThe right team for that job

For every intervention a tailor-made team is assembled: experienced installers, craftsmen and suppliers coordinated day by day under a single direction. In Milan as in Paris, in Maisach as in Klagenfurt. Whenever possible, without stopping the business.

05

Handover and follow-upOne reference, afterwards too

Every surface is checked before handover, and the point of contact stays the same after the opening. If anything is needed, the client doesn't have to search a supply chain for the responsible party: the reference is always one.
