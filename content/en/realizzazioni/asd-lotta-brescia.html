The project

The welcome
is the first round.

Whoever walks into a wrestling gym decides within seconds whether they are in the right place. For ASD Lotta in Brescia we focused the intervention where the eye lands first: the reception area. A dark, tactile three-dimensional feature wall and a bespoke counter with wood fronts and back-lit golden details.

All built with 3M DI-NOC finishes on existing or dry-built structures: no masonry, minimal downtime and the look of an exclusive club — on the budget of a restyling, not a renovation.
