The project

The same standard,
in six countries.

When an international brand renews its stores, the problem is never the single shop: it is repeating the same quality standard in Paris as in Nicosia, inside malls and on high streets, with stores open and retail seasons that won't wait.

For Guess we organised and directed restyling sites in eight European cities: panelling and fixtures wrapped in walnut-effect 3M DI-NOC, walls in concrete finish with the brand's vintage lettering, counters and fitting rooms renewed without demolishing anything. Every site with the same direction: survey, specification, 3M-certified installers selected locally or brought in where needed, site management and handover.

It is the project that best tells our model: no fixed crew to relocate, but a method that replicates anywhere — and one person answering for the result, in every city.
