The project

The same standard,
in nine countries.

When an international brand renews its stores, the problem is never the single shop: it is repeating the same quality standard in Paris as in Nicosia, inside malls and on high streets, with stores open and retail seasons that won't wait.

Between 2021 and 2023 Iconic Original coordinated the restyling of 19 Guess stores in 9 countries: panelling and fixtures covered in walnut-effect 3M DI-NOC finishes, walls in concrete finish with the brand's vintage lettering, counters and fitting rooms renewed without demolishing anything. Every site with the same direction: survey, specification, 3M-certified installers selected locally or brought in where needed, site management and handover.

It is the project that best tells the Iconic Original model: no fixed crew to relocate, but a method that replicates anywhere — and one point of contact following the result through, in every city.
