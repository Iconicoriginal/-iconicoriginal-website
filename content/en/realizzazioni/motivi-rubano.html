The project

A fashion salon inside a shopping mall.

Motivi puts a contemporary idea of femininity in its windows: the Rubano store had to tell it with clean surfaces, golden details and boutique lighting. We achieved it without demolishing anything: 3M DI-NOC wraps on the main surfaces, renewed displays and a fit-out that showcases the garments like a gallery.

The site, inside an active shopping mall, followed our rule: work fast and clean, coordinate every supplier, and hand the store back on the schedule agreed with the mall management.
