The project

A gym that motivates
at first sight.

In a gym the environment is part of the workout: the colours, the messages, the grit the walls transmit with every rep. For Mamiani in Pinerolo we built a high-energy visual identity — yellow and black, bold typography, motivational quotes — and brought it onto walls, locker rooms and lockers with bespoke wraps and graphics.

Here too, no demolition: the existing lockers were wrapped and numbered, the class rooms customised, the reception aligned with the new image. The facility kept training its members while, area by area, it changed its face.
