Why it works

A strategic alternative to traditional renovation.

With 3M wraps we completely transform surfaces, delivering a radical, long-lasting restyling with an extraordinary visual impact — at a fraction of the cost of traditional renovation. Using the best materials guarantees results that stand the test of time.

Renewing does not mean giving up what you have. There is no need to throw away furniture and objects that tell your story: we transform them, renew them, and integrate them with innovative design and high-quality materials. You keep the value and meaning of the things that are part of you, while giving your spaces new life and freshness.
