The method

One point of contact,
anywhere.

Other installers sell the crew they keep in-house, within the radius that crew can travel. I work the other way round: I start from your space and build the site around it — designers, 3M-certified installers, craftsmen — wherever that space happens to be. It is not a limitation: it is the model.

The difference

Just as an architect doesn't pour the concrete.

Nobody asks an architect to mix mortar: they ask them to answer for the building. That is exactly what I do. I take care of the design, choose the 3M DI-NOC materials finish by finish, select the best certified installers for each intervention and direct the works through to handover.

For you, everything changes: no chains of anonymous subcontractors, no responsibility bouncing around. One person answers for schedule, quality and result. I guarantee it myself.

The five phases
of the direction.

01

Survey and listeningIn person or remotely

I come to see the space — or we analyse it together with measurements and photos, if the site is on the other side of Europe. I understand what must stay, what must change and how your business works: the design starts there, not from a catalogue.

02

Design and specificationEvery detail decided upfront

I define the intervention surface by surface: what gets wrapped, what gets built, what gets integrated — drywall, lighting, floating floors where needed. You approve a clear project, with firm schedule and costs, before the site opens.

03

Material selection3M DI-NOC, sample in hand

I personally select the 3M DI-NOC finishes — woods, metals, stones, concretes, the sustainable E-Series RC and abrasion-resistant AR ranges — and check them on site, in the real light of your space. Only first-choice materials, installed to standard.

04

Works organised, anywhereThe right team for that job

I assemble the team tailored to your intervention and direct it day by day: "3M Endorsed" certified installers, craftsmen and suppliers coordinated by me. In Milan as in Paris, in Maisach as in Haifa. Whenever possible, without stopping your business.

05

Handover and single guaranteeOne name, even afterwards

I check every surface before handover and remain your only contact after opening. If anything is wrong, you don't have to hunt for the culprit along a supply chain: you call me. I answer for the result.
