Cookie Policy.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites send to the user's device, where they are stored to be sent back to the same sites on subsequent visits.

Cookies used by this site

Technical cookies and storage (always active). Required for the site to work: for instance, they remember your cookie consent choice (localStorage io-cookie-consent). They do not require consent.

Measurement cookies (only with consent). If enabled by the site owner and accepted via the banner, traffic measurement uses Google Analytics 4 with anonymised IP. Without consent, no measurement tool is loaded.

Third-party cookies

Pages embed Google Fonts web fonts served from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Loading the fonts does not install profiling cookies.

Managing preferences

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time by clearing this site's browsing data, or through your browser settings to block or delete cookies. For any request write to info@iconicoriginal.it.
