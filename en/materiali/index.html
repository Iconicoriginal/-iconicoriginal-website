The finishes

3M DI-NOC materials:
matter follows the design.

Over a thousand architectural finishes: woods, metals, stones, concretes, fabrics and the new sustainable series. 3M DI-NOC finishes reproduce matter with a realism you judge by touch — and they bond to almost any existing surface, without demolishing anything.

The difference is tactile.

Six families,
a thousand possibilities.

Each finish family is born for a precise use. These are the ones we choose most often on our sites — photographed in real projects, not in catalogues.

Wood finishes

The 3M DI-NOC wood range includes a vast selection spanning from traditional species, such as walnut, to rarer ones like mahogany and bubinga. These wraps create warm, welcoming and refined rooms, ideal for elegant spaces.

Pictured: Guess, Athens

Concrete and stucco

3M DI-NOC Concrete and Stucco finishes give an industrial, minimalist look that adapts to different styles, from contemporary to rustic. In the Guess store in Esch-sur-Alzette we used them to give the walls the memory of a vintage warehouse — without a gram of real concrete.

Pictured: Guess, Esch-sur-Alzette

Stone finishes

Respecting the budget while adding a touch of luxury, 3M DI-NOC Stone finishes make it possible to create rooms with natural charm: marbles, travertines and stones with book-matched veining, installed like real slabs.

Pictured: marble and wood composition

Metal finishes

The 3M DI-NOC metal series embraces the latest trends, including vibrant, textured, mesh and oxidised metal surfaces. Create modern, sophisticated rooms with an authentic final effect, with no extra costs or maintenance.

Pictured: ASD Lotta, Brescia

E-Series RC — Recycled Content

3M DI-NOC E-Series RC wraps offer innovative solutions for sustainability goals: elegant finishes in warm, matte, earth-inspired tones, made with seashell powder and recycled polyester, reducing environmental impact without compromising design.

3M certified sustainability

AR series — abrasion resistant

Extend the life of your projects in high-traffic environments with the AR series, engineered to resist wear. This finish protects your designs, keeping a flawless look even on heavily used spots: counters, edges, doors, handrails.

Pictured: Guess, Paris — wrapped edge

Every finish is worth as much as the hand that installs it: we are certified "3M Endorsed" installers and select accredited professionals from the same network for every site. The right material, installed to standard, guaranteed over time.
