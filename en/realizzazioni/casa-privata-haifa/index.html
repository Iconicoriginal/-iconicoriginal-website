The project

A design travels
lighter than concrete.

The project furthest from our home base is also the one that explains our model best. A private home in Haifa, Israel: three thousand kilometres from Val di Zoldo, another language, another way of building. With a fixed crew to relocate it would have been a gamble; with our direction it was a site like any other.

Design and specification prepared in Italy, 3M DI-NOC materials selected and shipped, installation coordinated on site: the living room walls dressed themselves in warm vertical wood slats, filtered by the Mediterranean light. Proof that demolition-free restyling knows no borders — not even geographic ones.

No geographic limits.
