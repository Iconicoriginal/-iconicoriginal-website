The project

The site starts in Val di Zoldo, lands near Munich.

Eis Café Alberto is an Italian story in Bavaria: a historic gelateria with its framed press clippings and loyal customers. The restyling had to renew it without erasing its identity — and it had to be organised 500 kilometres from our home base.

Which is exactly our craft: survey, design and specification from Italy, 3M DI-NOC materials selected in advance, installation team coordinated on site. Benches redone in deep red, light back-lit panelling, counters and bar wrapped, the venue's history celebrated like a manifesto. The result: a contemporary space that long-time customers immediately recognised as "their" café.
