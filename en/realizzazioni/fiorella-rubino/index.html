The project

A delicate identity,
replicated precisely.

The Fiorella Rubino image lives on powder tones, soft surfaces and curated details. Bringing it to different stores — a village outlet in Franciacorta, two malls in Campobasso and Brindisi — means performing the same miracle every time: transforming the existing space without stopping the mall living around it.

On the Fiorella Rubino sites we coordinated every phase: demolition avoided thanks to 3M DI-NOC wraps, drywall works, lighting, floors and final fit-out. The Brindisi site photos tell better than any words what "site direction" means: exposed systems, coordinated teams — and a few days later, a store ready to welcome its customers.
