The philosophy

Renew without destroying, transform with respect.

In these venues we brought all our experience to bear, giving value, identity and beauty back to places that welcome thousands of people every day.

We have worked in many cities, north to south, taking care of every detail: from installing 3M DI-NOC finishes — capable of radically changing the look of a space without demolishing anything — to directing the sites, coordinating every phase of the works. We personally selected the materials and oversaw the painting, the drywall works, the floating floors. All with a single goal: creating spaces consistent with the soul of each brand, functional and beautiful to live in.

These images are not just documentation of what was done. They reflect a philosophy: renew without destroying, enhance without distorting, transform with intelligence and respect. That is what we do with Iconic.
