The project

Mall sites: schedules are non-negotiable.

A store inside a shopping mall cannot afford weeks of closure: every day behind the shutters is lost revenue and penalties looming. For the Oltre stores we built fast, surgical sites, with the bulk of the transformation entrusted to 3M DI-NOC wraps: light warm surfaces, renewed counters, reconfigured fitting rooms.

In Erbusco the photos tell the whole sequence: the window closed by the "Coming soon" graphic, the counter under construction, then the store open and bright, as if it had always been that way. It is the transformation we like best: the one you never see coming.
