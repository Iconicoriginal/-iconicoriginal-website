The project

Renewing a dining room without turning off the stoves.

A restaurant is not just a venue: it is a daily ritual for its guests. For La Cala, in Viale Monte Nero in Milan, the restyling had to respect that ritual — no long closures, no dust, no distortion of the venue's Mediterranean soul.

With 3M DI-NOC wraps we renewed the dining room surfaces working in phases, during closing hours: arches, walls and furniture regained coherence and freshness, and the restaurant never stopped serving. The perfect example of what demolition-free restyling can do for hospitality.
