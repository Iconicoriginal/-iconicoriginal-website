Decorative panels

The magnetic
modular system.

Decorative panels that click onto a magnetic structure and swap in one gesture: the wall becomes a living surface, updated whenever the season, the collection or simply your taste changes. No masonry, no building site.

The wall that
changes its mind.

The system separates what stays from what can evolve: the structure is installed once, the panels with 3M DI-NOC finishes change as often as you like.

01

Permanent structure

Fixed once, on any existing wall.

04

Continuous updates

New functions and new looks added over time.

Who it is for

Retail, hospitality,
contract.

In stores, the modular system refreshes the image every season without touching the structure; in hotels and restaurants it allows maintenance and area-by-area restyling without closing; in offices it turns walls into reconfigurable surfaces. It is the natural complement to restyling: first we wrap, then we make the surface upgradeable forever.

The magnetic modular system is IconicWall, the product brand of the Iconic family: patented modular walls with magnetic panels, integrated light and functions.
